Frequently Asked Questions

Is DLNow free to use?

Yes, DLNow is completely free. There are no hidden charges or premium tiers.

What video qualities are available?

We offer multiple quality options including 1080p, 720p, and 480p for video. For audio, you can download in MP3 (320kbps or 128kbps) or M4A format.

Do I need to install anything?

No! DLNow works directly in your browser. Just paste a link and download — no software or extensions needed.

Is there a download limit?

To ensure fair usage, there's a rate limit of 10 requests per minute. This should be plenty for normal use.

Are my downloads private?

Yes. We don't store your URLs or files permanently. Downloaded files are automatically deleted from our servers within minutes.
